Course structure

A clear path to stronger chess

Foundation

6 months · Absolute beginners

Rating guide: 200–300
  • Board and piece fundamentals
  • Basic checkmates and tactics
  • Opening principles
  • Guided practice games and correction

Beginner

6 months · Familiar with the basic rules

Rating guide: 500–700
  • Forks, pins, skewers and tactics
  • Basic opening repertoire
  • Middlegame planning
  • Endgames and game analysis

Advanced Beginner

6–12 months

Rating guide: 1000–1200
  • White and Black opening preparation
  • Calculation and thinking process
  • Pawn structures and positional play
  • Tournament preparation

The rating ranges are course guidance, not promises of a FIDE rating.
